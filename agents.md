Shadow Ecology project operating rules

Before changing a model, residual definition, cross-validation design, null
model, cluster criterion, attribution method, or the March 2027 go/no-go
decision, read `pre_analysis_protocol.md` and
`governance_boundary_register.md`. Read `data_inventory.md` before acquiring,
substituting, or releasing a dataset.

`pre_analysis_protocol.md` is the authoritative record of the project's
confirmatory analysis. Do not alter its confirmatory rules after examining
model residuals or attribution results without adding a dated amendment that
states the change, its rationale, and whether it was made before or after
results were viewed.

Keep public and restricted data separate. Do not infer, publish, or expose
sensitive locations or non-public Tribal environmental information without
explicit authorization. Monitoring absence is a documented data gap; it is
not evidence that Tribal data or knowledge is absent.

Treat residuals as hypotheses about unmapped processes or data limitations,
not direct evidence of groundwater. Report competing explanations—especially
irrigation, salinity/playa conditions, geology, disturbance, terrain artifacts,
and sparse training support—alongside any hydrologic interpretation.

Before mapping, interpreting, publishing, or sharing results that identify a
priority area on or immediately adjacent to Tribal lands, follow the release
and consultation triggers in `governance_boundary_register.md`.
