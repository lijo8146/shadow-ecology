Dear Tribal leadership and environmental programs,

I am writing to introduce a research project focused on the Lower Walker
River–Walker Lake corridor that includes portions of the Walker River Paiute
Reservation. We are sharing this notice early because we recognize the
Reservation lies within our study area, and we wanted to be transparent about
what we are doing before any results are shared more broadly.

**What the project does:**

We are using publicly available satellite imagery (NASA/ESA) and federal land
cover and hydrography datasets (USGS, NRCS) to develop land cover maps of the
lower Walker Valley. The goal is to examine whether errors in those maps
contain information about ecological conditions that are not captured by
existing monitoring networks — for example, areas where groundwater may
influence vegetation in ways the public datasets do not reflect.

**What this project does NOT do:**

- We are not collecting any data on Tribal lands.
- We are not requesting any Tribal data, site locations, or ecological
  knowledge from the Tribe.
- We are not conducting field work, drone flights, or sampling on Tribal lands
  or waters.
- We are not using any data that is not publicly available from federal agencies.
- We are not making any determination about Tribal sovereignty, jurisdiction,
  land management, or environmental conditions.

**Why we are contacting you now:**

Any land cover analysis covering this corridor will include pixels on or near
Reservation lands, and we want to be open about that from the beginning.
Before we publish or share any result that characterizes specific locations
on or immediately adjacent to Reservation lands or waters, we intend to offer
the Tribe an opportunity to review the proposed maps and interpretation.

We are not requesting data or a Tribal endorsement. We simply want to make
sure the Tribe is aware of the project, and to ask whether there is an
appropriate office or representative we should address in future
correspondence.

**If you have questions or concerns:**

Please feel free to contact me at [PI email]. If the Tribe prefers not to
engage with this project, we fully respect that decision and will proceed
using public data only, continuing to apply the data-governance commitments
described above.
